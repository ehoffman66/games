How to play

Shooting

Steer with the joystick (WASD or arrows). Hold Shoot (Space) to load up, release to fire. Push the stick left or right as you release to pick a corner, or leave it centered and the shot goes for the open side. A quick tap is a fast, accurate wrister; a long hold is harder but less accurate.

Stickhandling

Push the joystick left and the puck sweeps to your backhand; push it right and it comes back to the forehand. Center the stick and the puck stays where it is. The goalie follows the puck, so quick dangles pull him across, and a shot loaded with the puck on your backhand is a real backhander you can aim anywhere. Tap Deke (Shift) with the stick sideways for a hard cut, with it centered for a toe drag that makes the goalie bite, or pulled back for a spin-o-rama. Tap it twice with the stick centered for the Michigan: the toe drag scoops the puck up onto your blade, lacrosse style, and Shoot tucks it in under the bar. Get it off quickly, before it falls off the blade. A goalie who drops into the butterfly or lunges with a poke can't reach it, but one who stays on his feet can still glove it. Backhanders are a bit slower but the goalie reads them late. Skate into the pads and he smothers it.

In net

Move your goalie with the joystick and stay between the puck and the net. Butterfly (Space) spreads your pads wide for a moment. Poke (Shift) strips a shooter who gets close, but a miss leaves you down and out.

Shootout rules

Five shooters each, alternating. No rebounds and no skating backward. Each attempt has a 8-second clock to get a shot off. Tied after five goes to sudden death.

Scouting

Sign a prospect from junior. Every one has a hidden potential, and about one in ten is a 95+ gem.

How players grow

Every attempt earns your shooter XP, and goals earn the most. Your goalie earns XP for every save. Each level gives a training point to raise a rating, and levels 3 and 6 unlock his signature perks. You can also train with coins any time, up to the player's potential. Players get a year older after every season: young ones keep growing, players past 30 slowly slip, and veterans retire.

What ratings do

Shot adds puck speed, Accuracy tightens your aim, Speed is top skating speed and Hands makes dekes quicker and harder. In net, Reflexes is lateral speed, Size covers more net, Poke wins more poke checks and Positioning makes him sharper overall. A 75 is average.
